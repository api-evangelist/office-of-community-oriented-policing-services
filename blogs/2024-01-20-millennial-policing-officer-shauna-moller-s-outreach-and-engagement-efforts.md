A graduate of George Mason University's Criminology Law and Society program and a former recipient of the Attorney General’s Award for Distinguished Service in Policing, Shauna Moller’s commitment to public safety with Manassas City Police has led her to enhance community engagement and awareness through initiatives such as Girls on the Run, domestic violence awareness, elder assistance, and other important social action programs.
