The safety and protection of our elderly loved ones is a concern that grows near and dear to many hearts across the nation. Oklahoma County Sheriff’s Office Deputy Tara Hardin takes this matter seriously in her service as the coordinator of the county’s TRIAD Program. The program is a collaborative effort between Oklahoma County’s local police departments and organizations that serve senior citizens; both groups work together to prevent and reduce senior victimization.
